# InvestAir Data

InvestAir gives your AI assistant direct access to proprietary ASX market intelligence —
cash runway, capital raises, substantial holders, broker activity, announcements and peer
analysis — through the [Model Context Protocol](https://modelcontextprotocol.io).

Ask questions in plain English. Your assistant calls the right tool, and answers are grounded
in InvestAir's data rather than the model's training set.

> *"Which ASX companies have between 2 and 3 quarters of cash runway?"*
>
> *"Show me capital raises in the gold sector over the last month."*
>
> *"Who are the substantial holders in this company, and which brokers do its peers use?"*

## Getting started

<Stepper>

1. **Subscribe**

   Choose a plan on the [Pricing](/pricing) page. You'll get an API key immediately —
   no credit card required during the trial.

1. **Connect your AI client**

   Open the [API Reference](/api) and find the **InvestAir Data** endpoint. It shows your
   MCP connection URL along with copy-paste setup instructions for Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor
   and VS Code.

   Your connection URL is the endpoint plus your key:

   ```
   https://investair-test-main-56df165.d2.zuplo.dev/mcp/prefect-v1?apiKey=YOUR_API_KEY
   ```

1. **Start asking questions**

   Once connected, the InvestAir tools appear in your assistant. Ask a question and it will
   pick the right one automatically.

</Stepper>

## Usage and limits

Your plan includes a monthly allowance of MCP requests. Track usage any time from your
subscription page — each tool call your assistant makes counts as one request.

:::tip{title="Keep your key private"}

Your API key sits in the connection URL, so treat that whole URL as a secret. If it's ever
exposed, contact us and we'll issue a replacement.

:::
