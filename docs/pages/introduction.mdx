Investair connects Claude and ChatGPT directly to Investair's proprietary ASX data, sourced
directly from announcements — cash runway, capital raises, substantial holders, broker
activity and peer analysis — through the [Model Context Protocol](https://modelcontextprotocol.io).

Ask questions and generate reports without leaving the assistant you already use. For Claude,
the Investair plugin also adds ready-made `/commands` and recurring scheduled reports.

> *"Which ASX companies have between 2 and 3 quarters of cash runway?"*
>
> *"Show me capital raises in the gold sector over the last month."*
>
> *"Who are the substantial holders in this company, and which brokers do its peers use?"*

## Getting started

<Stepper>

1. **Create your account and subscribe**

   Register on this site, then choose a plan on the [Pricing](/pricing) page. The trial is
   free — no credit card required.

1. **Connect your AI assistant**

   Open the [Connect](/connect) page for step-by-step setup. In Claude, install the Investair
   plugin from our marketplace; in ChatGPT, add our server as a custom connector.

1. **Start asking questions**

   Sign in when your assistant prompts you — same account as Step 1 — and the Investair tools
   appear. Ask a question and your assistant picks the right tool automatically.

</Stepper>

## Usage and limits

Your plan includes a monthly allowance of MCP requests. Track usage any time from your
subscription page — each tool call your assistant makes counts as one request.

:::tip{title="Signing in is all it takes"}

You don't need to copy or paste an API key anywhere. Your assistant signs you in securely the
first time you use an Investair tool, using the same account you registered here.

:::
